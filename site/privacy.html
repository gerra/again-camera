Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version. Again collects no data. Your photos stay on your device unless you choose to share them. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and we never see your photos.

Who we are

Again (on the stores as "Again Camera: Then & Now") is an independent app, built by one developer. "We" on this page means the people who make Again. Questions about this policy go to again@gerra.sh.

What we collect

Nothing. Again has no server and makes no network connections of its own. The Android app doesn't even have permission to use the internet. No photo, no setting and nothing about how you use the app is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

The "Privacy policy" link in the app opens this page in your browser, which then connects to this website like any other.

What stays on your device

  • The camera. Again uses the camera only while its camera screen is open, to show the viewfinder and take the photo you ask for. It asks your permission first.
  • The old photo. You choose it with the system photo picker, which hands Again only that one photo: the app can't see the rest of your library. Again keeps a private copy of the last old photo you chose, so that "Continue with this photo" works next time. Choosing another replaces it.
  • New photos. A new photo waits in the app's temporary storage until you save or share it. Photos you don't save are deleted the next time Again starts.
  • Saving. Save adds the new photo to your gallery: on Android to Pictures › Again, on iPhone and iPad to your photo library, after asking permission to add photos. Again can add to the library but not read it.
  • Location. Again doesn't ask for your location and doesn't add it to the photos it takes. The private copy of the old photo keeps whatever details the original file had, such as where it was taken, and stays on the device with it.

If your device's own backups are on (Google's backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone and iPad), they can include the app's private copy of the last old photo, under Google's or Apple's terms. Deleting Again deletes its storage from the device. Photos you saved stay in your gallery.

Sharing

Share opens your device's share sheet. The photo goes only to the app or person you pick there, and that app's own privacy policy covers what happens to it next. Again shares nothing by itself.

Google Play and the App Store

You get Again through Google Play or the App Store, under Google's or Apple's own terms and privacy policies. If you allowed your device to share diagnostics with app developers, they may show us anonymous statistics and crash reports for Again, such as how many devices it is installed on, or the device model and system version where it crashed. Those come from Google and Apple, not from the app, and contain none of your photos.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give you a copy of, correct or delete. Your photos are in your gallery and in the app's storage on your device, where you control them. If you have a question or a concern, email again@gerra.sh and we'll answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Again is a general-audience app and isn't directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If Again ever starts collecting anything, this page will say so before the version of the app that does it is released.
