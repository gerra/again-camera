Support

We read every message.

Email again@gerra.sh with what you tried, what happened, your phone model and its system version. A screenshot helps. We usually answer within a couple of days.

The camera doesn't open

Again needs permission to use the camera. If you said no earlier, turn it on in your phone's settings: on Android under Settings › Apps › Again › Permissions, on iPhone and iPad under Settings › Again. If the permission is on and the camera still can't be opened, another app may be using it (on iPad, check Split View): close it and try again.

Lining up the old photo

Stand where the old photo was taken, then pinch, drag and twist the old photo until it sits over the scene. The slider sets how strongly it shows, and Reset puts it back where it started. The old photo is only a guide on the screen: it never ends up in the new photo.

Where saved photos go

Only the photos you save are kept. On Android they are in your gallery under Pictures › Again; on iPhone and iPad, in your photo library. A new photo you don't save or share is deleted the next time Again starts.

An old photo won't open

Again opens ordinary photo files. If one can't be opened, choose another, or save a copy of it as a JPEG first. To bring in a paper print, take a photo of it with your camera app, then choose that.

Selfies come out mirrored

That's on purpose. The selfie camera's preview is a mirror, as people expect, and the photo is made the same way, so what you lined up is what you get.

Your photos and privacy

Again collects nothing and has no server: your photos stay on your device unless you share them. The privacy policy has the details.

Bugs and ideas

The code is on GitHub, and you can also report a bug or suggest something there as an issue.
