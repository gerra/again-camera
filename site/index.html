Pick a memory. Take it again.

A simple camera for recreating an old photograph from the exact same spot. The old photo floats over the live camera, you line the two up, and take the new one.

Coming to Google Play and the App Store.

Stand where it happened

Choose an old photo, find the spot, and let the camera help you line it up.

  • The old photo over the camera Half see-through, in a frame shaped like it. Pinch, drag and twist it into line; a slider sets how strongly it shows.
  • A clean photograph The old photo is a guide on the screen and never ends up in the new one.
  • Then and now Both in one frame, split by a divider to drag across.
  • Selfies too The selfie camera is a mirror, as people expect, and so is its photo: what you lined up is what you get.
  • Save, share or retake Retake keeps the old photo lined up where you left it.
  • Private by design No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Your photos stay on your phone (privacy).
